# Replacement direction derivation

Supersedes the rejected familiar-pharmacy-app candidate. Not a production design guide.

The mechanism is actual catalogue search, comparison and server-validated purchase for English/Arabic pharmacy shoppers in Ras Al Khaimah. Mobile use in ordinary daylight requires clear light surfaces and high-contrast controls. The rut is a pale rounded campaign card followed by repeated product shelves; its predictable opposite is a dark luxury editorial storefront. Neither leads this set.

1. **Care wayfinding** — A bold, searchable pharmacy service counter: direct search first, departments alongside.
2. **Care parcel** — An organised delivery parcel: item labels and a continuous shopping itinerary.
3. **Apothecary drawers** — Indexed department drawers unfold into dense product comparison.
4. **Family magazine** — Graphic family-care spreads lead discovery without pretending to be medical advice.
5. **Market arcade** — A bright digital shopping arcade: distinct departments, confident campaign windows and clear product shelves.
6. **Prescription ledger** — A precise catalogue index organises real products in consistent comparable rows.
7. **Daily routine** — A time-of-day care index leads into existing categories without prescribing treatment.

The recorded seed assigns candidate 5, Market arcade. Candidate 1 is the single independent pick. All six catalog challengers are offered as declined alternatives with explicit transferred disciplines. The owner may override the assignment. No implementation is authorised by a random selection.
