# Licensed product-photo candidates

A follow-up Open Beauty Facts lookup checked twelve reviewed cosmetic GTINs and returned five image candidates. This is additional to the earlier 35 unsuccessful lookups; it is not an exhaustive catalogue search. Each candidate's code was unique in the imported active catalogue. Labels were inspected for the 200 ml Sébium Gel moussant and the 200 ml Gel moussant actif. The selected 500 ml image is sideways; an alternative full photo was too tightly framed to show a complete pack. Do not treat every barcode hit as a suitable storefront photo.

The new source-credit component recognizes only canonical, check-digit-valid Open Beauty Facts product URLs. It displays the contributor attribution, a CC BY-SA 3.0 license link and a resizing notice beneath a real product gallery; placeholders and other providers receive no invented credit. Existing manufacturer and pharmacy images are unaffected. No new product metadata fields, medical classifications, prices or stock values are introduced by this UI change.

Evidence: [Open Food Facts licensing documentation](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side/), [publisher-managed image dataset license](https://github.com/awslabs/open-data-registry/blob/main/datasets/openfoodfacts-images.yaml), and [publisher's attribution guidance discussion](https://github.com/openfoodfacts/openfoodfacts-dart/issues/290). Keep the source URL, license evidence and upload receipt for each attached photo. Do not redistribute the broader data as an unlicensed proprietary dataset.

Verified metadata/source-code coverage and an isolated running Next.js credit-preview fixture confirmed the source/license links and photo rendering. That temporary fixture and its local photo were removed after verification. Actual catalogue associations must be applied only after the attribution release is live and the product identity, pack and image usage checks pass; candidate discovery is not proof of upload.
