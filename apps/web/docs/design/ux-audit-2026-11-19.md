# Al Mina storefront UI and UX audit

19 November 2026. Audited revision `da8998e` on `codex/storefront-pharmacy-app-reference`.

**Implementation integrity verdict: Pass.** The storefront expresses a coherent Al Mina system across the sampled shopping routes. Search, product identity, stock, dirham prices and buying actions are consistently prioritized. The earlier boxy controls are largely resolved. The remaining gaps cluster in expanded, sticky and optional states, where a small set of regressions matters more than another visual overhaul.

## P1 · Fix first

| Finding and evidence | Recommended change |
| --- | --- |
| **The fixed mobile product purchase bar is transparent.** At 390 px the 61 px dock computed to transparent in Chromium and WebKit in both locales. Product specifications are visible through the dock, including behind the unit price. This is a buying and text-legibility issue. [Mobile capture](implementation-evidence/ux-audit-2026-11-19/en-pdp-390.png), [fixed dock rule](../../src/components/product-detail.module.css#L313). | Restore an opaque retail surface only in fixed mode. Recheck default, added and error states at 320 and 390 px in both languages. Preserve the existing in-flow desktop treatment. Impeccable: `harden`. |
| **Search options create extra keyboard stops.** Arrow Down moves the active option while focus stays on the input, then Tab enters links nested inside the listbox options. Axe flags the nested interactive elements in both languages. Search is the primary shopping path. [Search widget](../../src/components/search-box.tsx#L49), [W3C combobox pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/combobox/). | Use one combobox keyboard model with one selectable element per option. Keep the popup options out of the page Tab sequence. Retest arrow selection, Enter, Escape, mouse, touch and screen-reader announcement. Impeccable: `harden`. |
| **Optional-report consent uses low-contrast white text and English-only copy.** Axe measured **3.05:1** on the 14 px Allow button when reporting was enabled, compared with the **4.5:1** minimum. The Arabic cart also rendered the main preference section and buttons in English. [Preference component](../../src/components/usage-preferences.tsx#L33), [WCAG 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html). | Use the storefront action colors and localize the full consent block, including errors and status text. Verify both allow and decline states without changing the existing opt-in semantics. Impeccable: `colorize` then `clarify`. |
| **Blank filter inputs are hard to locate.** The price inputs have transparent borders and a measured **1.06:1** fill-to-page contrast. For inputs that rely on their boundaries, WCAG calls for a **3:1** identifying cue. The labels and magnifier help with recognition, and focus is visible. The price entry areas are the clearest failure risk at rest. [Open filter capture](implementation-evidence/ux-audit-2026-11-19/en-filters-390.png), [field styles](../../src/components/catalog-list.module.css#L124), [WCAG 1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html). | Add a quiet, sufficiently contrasting input edge or baseline. Keep the open filter rail and avoid outlining every chip or panel. Verify default, focus and invalid states. Impeccable: `colorize`. |

## Keep these strengths

- Search is easy to reach, the mobile home presents discovery and the first purchase row within 390 × 844, and desktop has a usable five-card shelf.
- Product cards separate price, stock and availability, use a consistent tap-sized Add action, and keep pack identity visible. The product page preserves the image-first mobile layout.
- English and Arabic preserved writing direction, and the sampled routes had no document-level horizontal overflow. The measured 40-step keyboard path through the mobile listing did not hide a focused main-content control under the nav. Cart and checkout preserve the primary action across viewport changes.
- The sampled loading and empty states keep the way back to shopping visible. The phone checkout keeps manual address entry available.

## Coverage and detector interpretation

- Inspected nine route surfaces, English and Arabic, at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px. Playwright recorded **84 route and layout observations** in total, including two expanded filter observations and ten WebKit spot checks. There were **38 axe page or filter scans**, plus two expanded search scans. Dedicated probes measured the mobile dock, checkout option widths and telemetry contrast in Chromium and WebKit. The open-search and consent findings are state-dependent.
- The audit used the loopback reference catalogue for pack and homepage visuals, and an isolated compiled fixture for checkout and performance. The fixture presents sample stock and prices. The optional-report finding was observed with reporting enabled in the fixture. Physical devices, screen-reader audio, live search relevance, live services and field performance remain outside this audit.
- Impeccable emitted 43 advisory matches across the storefront and component directories. Most were literal font sizes or map colors and radii. Some literals duplicate known scale values, some sit in compatibility styles and several were from admin, outside this audit. The inspected `/map` route resolved to contact in the fixture, so the optional map styling matches were kept as source observations. The count is neither a defect count nor evidence of 43 shopper-facing violations.

## Proposed order

1. `$impeccable harden` for the mobile buying dock and search popup semantics.
2. `$impeccable colorize` and `$impeccable clarify` for control identifiability and localized consent.
3. `$impeccable adapt` and `$impeccable clarify` for checkout. Use `$impeccable distill` on the empty Offers layout and `$impeccable optimize` to investigate route payloads.
4. `$impeccable polish` for the reviewed hero and catalogue imagery, followed by a repeat audit of the changed states.

This is an audit of the approved storefront. Application code and deployment state were untouched.
