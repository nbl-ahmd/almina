## Product and users

Al Mina is an existing pharmacy ecommerce application serving customers in Ras
Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. Customers browse actual products/categories,
search offers, inspect product details, save product quantities in their cart
and review delivery and server-calculated totals at checkout. Administration,
inventory and order management are existing production workflows.

Product names, prices, availability, categories, images and descriptions come
from the application. Customers must be able to find products, compare options,
understand current prices/availability and complete the existing shopping flow.
Labels must describe actual actions; never invent claims, reviews, discounts or
services. Preserve English/Arabic content, locale switching and RTL behavior,
accessible keyboard/touch controls, loading/empty/error states and existing SEO.

## Current redesign brief

The owner supplied three reference images on 15 November 2026 and requested a
new storefront system and staged responsive redesign. The owner selected image 1 for
product cards, image 2 for the gallery-first product detail, and image 3 for the
homepage and sticky purchase bar. They requested the current logo colours, an
app-like mobile experience, better integrated navigation/search/address selection,
and a deliberate desktop adaptation. Review uses working coded previews. See `docs/design/storefront/references/README.md` for source
and interpretation boundaries. The retired visual identity has no authority.
Palette/type/layout decisions belong to the new design specification, not this
product record. Existing shopping capabilities and production safeguards remain.

## Production constraints

Production safety, security, server-authoritative commerce and backward
compatibility take precedence over visual changes. Development verification
uses synthetic loopback fixtures; no deployment or production database change
is authorized by a UI task. Root AGENTS.md owns the detailed guardrails.

## Brand commitments

Keep Al Mina Pharmacy’s real name, logo and contact details. The owner explicitly
selected the logo’s coral/aqua colour family for this redesign. Source screenshots
inform composition and interaction; their claims, prices, ratings, fictional
products and commercial promises are not Al Mina facts. The retired system is
not the authority for the new implementation.
